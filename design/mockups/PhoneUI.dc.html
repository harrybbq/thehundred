THE HUNDRED · PHONE UI v2
Same noir, built for drunk thumbs. Every screen asks one question and has one big key with one verb. A "NOW" strip at the top of Home says what's happening and what you should do. Keys are 64px or taller (the main one 88px, full width) with 12px or more between them. Anything that can't be undone is a two-step on the screen: tap a face, then "YES". There are no long-presses, drags or small toggles, and every tap answers back with a press, a flash and a click. Nothing on Home gives your role away: the file and the Moves case look the same on every phone.
