# TV summons clips: names B

Names covered here: Emma, Matthew, Thomas, Munro, Jenkins, Luke, Jimmy, Eva.
(Aaron, Harry, Aidan, Sol, Joshua, Jamie, Kyle, James and Beth are in the other agent's file.)

This file is research only. Nothing has been downloaded or ripped.

**About licences:** unless a moment says otherwise, it is copyrighted (studio, label or broadcaster). Playing a 1–3s clip at a private house party is low-risk in practice, but it is not "licensed", and the clips must not be bundled into a public build or repo. The only genuinely free options found are **LibriVox** audiobook recordings. They are public domain in the USA, so check UK status, though most of these texts are long out of copyright. They are noted where they help.

**About timestamps:** where a timestamp says "approx" it was not checked frame by frame. Scrub the linked clip before cutting.

## Emma

1. **"WHO'S EMMA?!" (Nicole Samuel), *Love Island* UK, series 11 (ITV2, 2024)**, meme / TV
   - Line: "Who's Emma?" / "Who's EMMARRR?", **shouted** in a strong Welsh accent during the Casa Amor "Raunchy Race" challenge, when she learns that Ciaran kissed Emma Milton.
   - Clip: YouTube "'Who's Emma?' The Casa Chaos!" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KjbS8Jy3jrQ (clip timestamp not checked). The TikTok sound is everywhere: https://www.tiktok.com/discover/nicole-saying-who-is-emma
   - Usable: about 1.0s. The name is the punchline, so it works perfectly as "who's Emma? → *that* Emma, get up".
   - Why: a huge UK viral sound (about 4k TikTok uses) that every Love Island viewer will recognise. Source: https://www.capitalfm.com/news/tv-film/love-island/whos-emma-tiktok-sound-explained/
   - Licence: © ITV.
2. **Hot Chocolate, "Emma" (1974, UK #3)**, song
   - Line: Errol Brown **sings** and cries out "Emma… Emmaline". The anguished "Emma!" near the end is the bit to use.
   - Clip: find it on YouTube or Spotify ("Hot Chocolate Emma"). Timestamp not verified; the cry is in the final third.
   - Usable: about 1.5s.
   - Why: a classic British pop track. Note that the song is about suicide, so keep only the name and none of the lyrics around it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_(Hot_Chocolate_song)
   - Licence: © label.
3. **FREE: LibriVox *Emma* (Jane Austen), chapter 1 opening**, audiobook
   - Line: "**Emma** Woodhouse, handsome, clever, and rich…", **said** (the first word of the book).
   - Source: https://librivox.org/emma-by-jane-austen-solo/ (also versions 3, 4 and 5, and a full-cast dramatic reading on archive.org)
   - Usable: about 0.6s for "Emma", or about 3s for the whole posh phrase, which is funny read out at a drinking game.
   - Licence: **public domain (LibriVox)**.

## Matthew

1. **"Tonight, Matthew, I'm going to be…", *Stars in Their Eyes* (ITV, Matthew Kelly era 1993–2004)**, TV
   - Line: contestants **say** "Tonight, Matthew, I'm going to be [star]" before walking through the smoke doors.
   - Clips: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqC_M3ZFnsI (2000 episode) and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8SqRZBrqfFI (2004). Each contestant's line comes just before the doors, every few minutes. GIF: https://tenor.com/view/stars-in-their-eyes-tonight-matthew-gif-17047785474267713671
   - Usable: about 1.5s for "Tonight, Matthew…". Cut before the star's name; the cliffhanger works as a summons.
   - Why: one of the most famous UK TV catchphrases, and every British adult knows it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stars_in_Their_Eyes
   - Licence: © ITV/Granada.
2. **Cat Stevens, "Matthew and Son" (1967, UK #2)**, song
   - Line: "**Matthew and Son**", **sung** as the hook.
   - Clip: YouTube "Cat Stevens Matthew and Son". The hook comes at the chorus (approx 0:20–0:30).
   - Usable: about 1.5s.
   - Why: a well-known 60s UK hit with a punchy, clear name. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_and_Son
   - Licence: © label.
3. **FREE fallback: "Matthew, Mark, Luke and John" (traditional bedtime prayer)**, or a LibriVox reading of the Gospel of St Matthew ("The Gospel According to Saint Matthew"). The text is public domain. A LibriVox recording would be PD; find one via librivox.org search. This option is weak on comedy.

## Thomas

1. **"THOMAS!" (Mammy Two Shoes), *Tom and Jerry* (MGM, 1940–52)**, TV/cartoon
   - Line: Mammy **shouts** "Thomas!" at Tom (his full name) whenever he is in trouble.
   - Clip: "Mammy Two Shoes – 'Thomas!! Get off of that bed!'" (*Sleepy-Time Tom*, 1951) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Xu22H2ujdc (the shout is right at the start). More in this playlist: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLWjzRAVkRjM-NWh2bX1ZF6ONXXaFMfXCw
   - Usable: about 0.8s, and loud and clear.
   - Why: a perfect "you're in trouble" summons that UK kids grew up with. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mammy_Two_Shoes
   - Licence: © Warner Bros. **Caution:** the Mammy Two Shoes character is a racial caricature and was redubbed or reanimated on later TV runs. Use the June Foray redub version (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dx2qPwIHMFU) or skip it if this feels off.
2. **"Engine Roll Call", *Thomas & Friends* (end song, series 8–21, from 2004)**, TV song
   - Line: "**Thomas**, he's the cheeky one…", **sung**. The original 1984 Ringo-era theme (Mike O'Donnell & Junior Campbell) is **instrumental** and never sings "Thomas", so it only works as a music bed.
   - Lyrics: https://ttte.fandom.com/wiki/Engine_Roll_Call. Clip: YouTube "Thomas Engine Roll Call". "Thomas" is the first name sung after the intro (approx 0:10).
   - Usable: about 0.7s for "Thomas", or 2s with "…he's the cheeky one".
   - Why: instant childhood nostalgia, and the theme is a meme in its own right ("Thomas the Dank Engine").
   - Licence: © Mattel/HIT.
3. **Combined idea:** play the instrumental Thomas theme's first bar (about 2s) as the sting and show the name on screen. This is the most recognisable option even without the word.

## Munro

Nothing great exists for the exact name, but the **homophone "Monroe" works**, since Munro is pronounced mun-ROE.

1. **Madonna, "Vogue" (1990, UK #1), rap break**, song
   - Line: "Greta Garbo and **Monroe**, Dietrich and DiMaggio…", **spoken/rapped** clearly over a beat.
   - Clip: official video on YouTube. The rap starts at approx 3:50 in the single edit (check it).
   - Usable: about 0.8s for "and Monroe".
   - Why: everyone knows the rap and will join in. Source: https://www.biography.com/musicians/madonna-vogue-celebrities-rap
   - Licence: © Sire/Warner.
2. ***The Last of the Mohicans* (1992)**, film
   - Line: Colonel Edmund **Munro** (played by Maurice Roëves) is addressed as "Colonel Munro" throughout. Magua calls him "the Grey Hair".
   - Clip: search YouTube for "Last of the Mohicans Colonel Munro" (exact timestamp not found). Quotes: https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/The_Last_of_the_Mohicans_(1992_film)
   - Usable: about 1s. It is said in normal dialogue, not iconic. Use it over the famous "Promentory" theme music for drama.
   - Licence: © 20th Century.
   - **FREE:** LibriVox *The Last of the Mohicans* (Cooper, 1826) says "Munro" constantly. Public domain.
3. **Scottish angle (no audio found):** a "Munro" is a Scottish mountain over 3,000ft, and "Munro bagging" is climbing them. A bagpipe sting plus "MUNRO" on screen is a decent fallback.
- Verdict: **use Vogue's "…and Monroe"**. There is no famous shouted "Munro!" moment.

## Jenkins

1. **"LEEEROOOY JENKINS!", *World of Warcraft* machinima "Leeroy!!" (Pals for Life guild, May 2005)**, game/meme
   - Line: **shouted** battle cry after "Alright chums, let's do this." He charges into the Rookery (Upper Blackrock Spire).
   - Clip: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pz6vl0ZjOL8 (original) and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hooKVstzbz0 (HD). The shout is roughly two-thirds through (approx 1:25–1:35, scrub to check).
   - Usable: about 2.0–2.5s for "LEEEROY JENKINS" (cut before the "oh my God, he just ran in").
   - Why: one of the most famous memes ever, and a charge-in battle cry is perfect for being called up. Bonus: "At least I have chicken" for a loss. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeroy_Jenkins and https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/leeroy-jenkins
   - Licence: fan-made machinima using Blizzard game assets, so © Blizzard/creators. It is widely reshared, but not free.
2. No strong second "Jenkins" moment was found. Fallback: the "Leeroy" shout alone, or a 0.5s cut of just "JENKINS!".

## Luke

1. **"Use the Force, Luke", *Star Wars* (A New Hope, 1977), Death Star trench run**, film
   - Line: Obi-Wan (Alec Guinness) **says** "Use the Force, Luke", spoken as an echoing whisper. Later: "Luke, trust me."
   - Clip: official https://www.starwars.com/video/use-the-force-luke. YouTube copies are easy to find.
   - Usable: about 1.5s. It is soft, so it needs a volume boost.
   - Licence: © Lucasfilm/Disney.
2. **The "Luke, I am your father" misquote, *The Empire Strikes Back* (1980)**, film
   - **The real line is "No. I am your father."** "Luke" is not in it, so you can't cut "Luke" from the famous moment. Vader does say "**Luke**, you can destroy the Emperor… join me" right after, which is usable as about 0.5s for "Luke".
   - Fun option: play Vader's "No… I am your father" (about 2s) as the sting and show "LUKE" on screen. The crowd will "hear" the misquote anyway.
   - Licence: © Lucasfilm/Disney.
3. **Return of the Jedi (1983)**: "**Luke**… help me take this mask off" (Vader, said weakly), about 0.6s for "Luke". It is also covered by the PD "Matthew, Mark, Luke and John" prayer (see Matthew).

## Jimmy

1. **The Undertones, "Jimmy Jimmy" (1979, UK #16)**, song
   - Line: "**Jimmy Jimmy**", **sung** as a chant-like chorus hook. The band are from Derry, which suits a UK crowd.
   - Clip: Top of the Pops, 17 May 1979 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=66CCLS0do7c. The first chorus is at approx 0:30 (check it).
   - Usable: about 1.5s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Jimmy_(song)
   - Licence: © label.
2. **"Jimmy Jimmy Aaja Aaja", *Disco Dancer* (Bollywood film, 1982)**, film song/meme
   - Line: "**Jimmy Jimmy Jimmy**, aaja aaja aaja", **sung** (Parvati Khan; music by Bappi Lahiri). It was a giant TikTok meme in 2022.
   - Clip: GIF and sound https://tenor.com/view/jimmy-jimmy-jimmy-jimmy-aaja-aaja-aaja-aaja-aaja-jimmy-jimmy-gif-27576520. The YouTube song video opens on the hook within the first seconds.
   - Usable: about 2.5s for "Jimmy Jimmy Jimmy aaja". It is the funniest of the three for drunk people.
   - Licence: © Saregama.
3. **Madonna, "Vogue"**: "Marlon Brando, **Jimmy Dean**", spoken in the same rap as the Munro clip (about 0.7s). Martha & the Vandellas' "**Jimmy Mack**" (1967) is an alternative sung option.

## Eva

1. **"Eee-vah!", *WALL-E* (Pixar, 2008)**, film
   - Line: WALL-E **says** EVE's name as "Eeee… vah?". It is a running gag, and he calls "EVA!" repeatedly through the film.
   - Clip: "WALL-E Meets Eva" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GyBeACKZOU4 (the name exchange is in the second half of the clip). Supercut: "Evaaaa Wall-EEEE" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1JVw8eofx8Q
   - Usable: 0.8–1.2s. It is clear, cute and instantly recognisable. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WALL-E_(character)
   - Licence: © Disney/Pixar.
2. ***Evita* (musical, 1978; film 1996)**: the crowd chants "**Evita!**" and "**Eva** Perón" in the songs. **Not verified** which track gives the cleanest "Eva". Weaker than WALL-E.
3. **Fallback:** a robot-voice "EVA" gag pairs well with the WALL-E clip anyway. No free recording was found.
