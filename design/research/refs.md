# Reference board: Walk the Plank and Dodge

Screenshots taken in Chrome on 29 Sept 2026. Each line says what technique to take. **Never copy the artwork itself.**
Everything we ship is drawn by us in SVG or CSS.

`current/` holds screenshots of our own mockups, taken before any of these changes.
